## LinkedIn (limit 3,000 characters)

A signature is a promise. I built Signet to make it count.

Most e-signing stops at "a name on a line." For agreements that matter, you also need to prove who signed, what they agreed to, and that nothing changed afterwards. Signet handles that end to end:

• Identity checks: every recipient verifies control of their email with a one-time code before they can sign.
• Clear consent: the exact consent statement is recorded with each signature.
• Private by default: documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, a fresh key per file, before they reach local or cloud storage.
• A clear record: the signed PDF is sealed, with a hash-chained audit trail and a signing certificate signed by the issuer (Ed25519).
• Independent checks: anyone can verify a sealed document's fingerprint on a public page, without exposing signer contact details.

Built with Next.js, MongoDB and pdf-lib. From first page to final proof.
