# Signet — brag plan

**What it is:** An e-signing platform that verifies who signed, records clear consent, and seals the PDF with a tamper-evident audit certificate.
**For:** People sending agreements that matter, who need to prove later who signed what, and when.
**Sets it apart:** It doesn't stop at a signature: email one-time-code identity checks, the exact consent statement, a hash-chained audit trail, and a public seal-verification page.
**Most impressive claim:** "A tamper-evident audit trail and signed certificate travel with every completed file."
**Visual hook:** A signature drawing itself on a rule, then the real hero line: "A signature is a promise. Make it *count.*"
**Real UI shown:** The landing-page document card and status card, the signing page's email verification card (OTP), the "Sign in the box" signature card with the real consent text, and the verify page's "Seal verified." result.
**Tone:** `polished`. Serious, calm and confident, because it's a trust product. Long holds, soft staggered transitions.
**Share caption:** "A signature is a promise. Signet makes it count: verified signers, recorded consent, sealed PDFs."

**Identity:** paper `#f8f8f4`, ink `#17201d`, green `#244c3c`, green-light `#e5eee8`, muted `#68736d`. Serif headlines (Georgia → Noto Serif fallback), sans body, `s` brand mark and logo.png.
**Fictional stand-ins:** signer "Jordan Ellis" (already the landing page's sample), OTP `482916`, and an illustrative SHA-256 fingerprint. No real data.

## Music
120 BPM (beat 0.5s, bar 2s), D major, 20 bars. Soft pad + bell arpeggio from frame 0; kick/hat/bass enter at the reveal (4.0s); the last bars resolve on Dmaj and ring out. Every cut and SFX sits on the beat grid. Music ducks ~14 dB under each voiceover line.
